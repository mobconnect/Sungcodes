# Sungcodes privacy notice

Sungcodes is a static web application. The lyrics studio runs in your browser and does not send your prompts or generated drafts to a Sungcodes server. The most recent draft is stored in your browser's local storage so it can be restored on the same device; clear your browser storage to remove it.

The artist library is loaded from the public GitHub Contents API. GitHub may process requests according to its own privacy policy and terms. External links, including GitHub and justbeyou.com.au, have their own policies.

Do not enter sensitive, personal, confidential or copyrighted material you do not have permission to use.
